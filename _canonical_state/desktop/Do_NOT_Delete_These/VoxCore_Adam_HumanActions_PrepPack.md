Before any acquirer outreach, Adam should know:

| Bucket | Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Checking + savings (immediate) | bank apps | Covers M&A attorney retainer ($5-10K), tax advisor, courier costs |

**Target:** $25K accessible inside 30 days, of which $15K is liquid cash. Below this, the acquirer-cooperation effort is funded on credit.

### TSP Loan Eligibility — pull the number; don't draw

Action: log into TSP.gov, navigate to "Loans" → "Loan Calculator." Note the maximum loan available. **Do not initiate a loan.** This is just to know the backstop.

Why not draw: TSP loans must be repaid before separation or they're treated as taxable distributions. Active separation pending = bad timing for a TSP loan.

### Personal Loan Pre-Shop — soft pull, no commitment

Three rate-shop options, all soft-pull (no credit-score impact for the inquiry):

- SoFi Personal Loan — pre-qualify online, ~30 sec
- LightStream — pre-qualify online
- Marcus by Goldman Sachs — pre-qualify online

**What to record:** APR offered, max term, max amount, any origination fees. Do not accept; just gather rates.

### ❓ Funding Path Decision — frame for Adam

Five paths in priority order based on cost-to-borrow:

1. Cash savings (cost: 0%, ceiling: liquid cash)
2. 0% APR card (cost: 0% during intro, ~22% after; ceiling: card limit)
3. TSP loan (cost: G-fund interest rate, ~4-5%; ceiling: TSP balance × 0.5)
4. Personal loan (cost: 8-15% depending on credit; ceiling: lender)
5. Family loan (cost: relationship; ceiling: relationship)

**Default ladder for ~$25K M&A attorney retainer:** $10K cash → $10K 0% card → $5K family loan if needed. TSP loan and personal loan stay in reserve.

### ❓ Acceptable Retention Length

| Length | Pro | Con |
|---|---|---|
| 24 months | Shorter lock; faster to next move | Lower upfront cash, less acquirer commitment |
| 36 months | Higher cash + equity; standard | Longer commitment |
| 48+ months | Maximum total comp | Too long; opportunity cost grows |

Industry default for acquihire is 24-36 months. Adam owns the upper bound based on family / quality-of-life calculus.

### ❓ Cash vs Equity Preference — frame

Liquidity vs upside trade-off. Active-duty separation = limited runway for illiquid equity. Recommendation framework:
- 60% cash / 40% equity if acquirer is pre-IPO
- 70% cash / 30% equity if acquirer is post-Series-C and equity is illiquid
- 50% cash / 50% equity if acquirer is post-IPO and equity is publicly tradable
Adam picks the percentages; the framework helps anchor the negotiation.

### ❓ Walk-Away Number

**Define the floor:** below what total deal value would Adam decline?

Frame: total comp = (signing cash) + (year-1 cash) + (vesting equity over retention) + (benefits delta vs. self-employed/government). A walk-away under $750K total over 24 months for a single-developer acquihire is below market for the systems VoxCore actually demonstrates. Above $1.5M is the upper anchor. Adam fills in the specific number.
